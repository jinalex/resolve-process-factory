# KYB Onboarding Review: Level 1 Standard Operating Procedure

Harbor Sponsor Bank, Partner Banking Operations. Version 1.0. (Fictional bank. Section 3 follows FinCEN's CDD rule, 31 CFR 1010.230.)

## 1. Purpose

L1 analysts review each business (legal entity) applying for an account through a fintech partner. Every application receives exactly one outcome:

- **Approve**: all requirements below are met and no risk factor applies.
- **Incomplete**: a required fact has not been established yet. The case goes back for more investigation.
- **Request documents**: ask the customer for missing or stale documents.
- **Escalate**: refer to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).
- **Decline**: do not open the account.

## 2. General principles

2.1. When more than one section applies, the most severe outcome governs: Decline, then Escalate, then Request documents, then Incomplete, then Approve.

2.2. A fact that has not been established is unknown, not false. If any fact required by this procedure is unknown, the case cannot be approved.

## 3. Beneficial ownership

3.1. Entities on the excluded list (for example banks, SEC-registered issuers and registered investment companies) do not require beneficial owners to be identified.

3.2. For every other entity, identify one control person: a single individual with significant responsibility to control, manage or direct the entity. If no control person is identified, request documents.

3.3. Unless the entity is excluded or is a nonprofit, identify every individual who owns 25% or more of the entity, directly or indirectly. Ownership is complete only when no unidentified person could own 25% or more, given the shares that are unaccounted for or held through entities whose owners are unknown. If ownership is not complete, request documents.

3.4. Verify the identity of every identified individual. If any is unverified, request documents.

3.5. The entity's registry filing must be less than 12 months old. Otherwise request documents.

## 4. Screening

4.1. A confirmed sanctions match on the entity or any identified individual: decline.

4.2. A potential sanctions match that has not been cleared: escalate.

4.3. A politically exposed person among the identified individuals: escalate.

4.4. Adverse media on the entity or any identified individual: escalate.

## 5. Industry

5.1. Prohibited industries (marijuana-related business, adult content, unlicensed money transmission): decline.

5.2. Restricted industries (money services businesses, virtual currency): escalate.

5.3. Licensed money services businesses whose state licence has been verified may be approved.

## 6. Geography

6.1. The entity or any identified individual is located in a comprehensively sanctioned country: decline.

6.2. The entity or any identified individual is located in a FATF high-risk or increased-monitoring jurisdiction: escalate.
