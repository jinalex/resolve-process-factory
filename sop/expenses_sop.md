# Employee Expense Claims: Review Procedure

Fernhill Analytics Ltd, Finance Operations. Version 1.0. (Fictional company. A second, small SOP used to check that Resolve's core works outside KYB. Amounts are in US dollars.)

## 1. Purpose

Finance reviewers check each expense claim an employee submits. A claim is a list of lines, one per receipt or expense. Every claim receives exactly one outcome:

- **Approve**: pay the claim.
- **Incomplete**: a required fact has not been established yet. The reviewer finds it before deciding.
- **Return**: send the claim back to the employee to fix and resubmit.
- **Escalate**: refer to the Finance Director.
- **Reject**: do not pay the claim.

## 2. General principles

2.1. When more than one section applies, the most severe outcome governs: Reject, then Escalate, then Return, then Incomplete, then Approve.

2.2. A fact that has not been established is unknown, not false. If any fact required by this procedure is unknown, the claim cannot be approved.

## 3. The claim

3.1. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the earliest expense on them. Reject a claim submitted 61 days or more after its earliest expense.

3.2. A claim's approver must not be the employee who submitted it. Reject a self-approved claim.

3.3. A claim totalling $5,000 or more needs the Finance Director's sign-off. Without it, escalate.

## 4. Each line

4.1. Every line of $75 or more needs an itemised receipt. If a receipt is missing or has not been attached, return the claim.

4.2. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Return any claim with a line for alcohol.

4.3. A gift to a government official must be reviewed under the anti-bribery policy. Escalate any claim with such a line.

4.4. Business-class and first-class airfare must be reviewed. Escalate any claim with such a line.

4.5. Business-class airfare pre-approved in writing by the Finance Director may be approved.
