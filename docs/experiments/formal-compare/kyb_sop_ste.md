# KYB Onboarding Review: Level 1 Procedure (STE100 strict rewrite of kyb_sop.md)

Glossary: "true sanctions match" means a sanctions match that the screening team marked as the same person or entity. "Identified individual" means a beneficial owner or the control person.

## 1. Purpose

The L1 analyst reviews each business that applies for an account through a fintech partner. Give each application one outcome:

- **Approve**: The application meets all requirements in this procedure. No risk factor applies.
- **Incomplete**: The analyst did not establish a required fact. Send the case back for more investigation.
- **Request documents**: Ask the customer for missing documents or old documents.
- **Escalate**: Send the case to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).
- **Decline**: Do not open the account.

## 2. General principles

2.1. More than one section can apply to an application. Use the outcome with the highest severity. The order of severity, from highest to lowest, is:

1. Decline
2. Escalate
3. Request documents
4. Incomplete
5. Approve

2.2. If the analyst did not establish a fact, the fact is unknown. An unknown fact is not false. Do not approve an application if a required fact is unknown.

## 3. Beneficial ownership

3.1. Some entities are on the excluded list. Examples are banks, SEC-registered issuers and registered investment companies. Do not identify beneficial owners for an entity on this list.

3.2. For all other entities, identify one control person. The control person is one individual with significant responsibility to control, manage or direct the entity. If you cannot identify a control person, request documents.

3.3. If the entity is not on the excluded list and is not a nonprofit, identify each individual who owns 25% or more of the entity. Include direct ownership and indirect ownership. Some shares can have no known owner. Some shares can belong to an entity with owners that you do not know. Ownership is complete only if no unidentified person can own 25% or more through these shares. If ownership is not complete, request documents.

3.4. Verify the identity of each identified individual. If you cannot verify one identity, request documents.

3.5. The registry filing of the entity must be less than 12 months old. If the filing is 12 months old or older, request documents.

## 4. Screening

4.1. If the entity or an identified individual has a true sanctions match, decline the application.

4.2. If the entity or an identified individual has a possible sanctions match, and the screening team did not clear it, escalate the application.

4.3. If an identified individual is a politically exposed person, escalate the application.

4.4. If the entity or an identified individual has adverse media, escalate the application.

## 5. Industry

5.1. If the entity operates in a prohibited industry, decline the application. The prohibited industries are marijuana-related business, adult content and unlicensed money transmission.

5.2. If the entity operates in a restricted industry, escalate the application. The restricted industries are money services businesses and virtual currency.

5.3. If the entity is a licensed money services business, and you verified its state licence, you may approve the application.

## 6. Geography

6.1. If the entity or an identified individual is in a comprehensively sanctioned country, decline the application.

6.2. If the entity or an identified individual is in a FATF high-risk jurisdiction or increased-monitoring jurisdiction, escalate the application.
