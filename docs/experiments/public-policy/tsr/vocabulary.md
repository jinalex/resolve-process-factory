# What a case supplies

Listed by Claude from the procedure (step `facts`), before any case was read.

## The case

- `induces_purchase_by_telephone` (yes / no): Was the sale made through a plan, program or campaign run to induce the purchase of goods or services by use of one or more telephones? Yes if it was.
- `more_than_one_interstate_call` (yes / no): Does the plan, program or campaign involve more than one interstate telephone call? Yes if it does.
- `solicited_through_mailed_catalog` (yes / no): Was the sale solicited through the mailing of a catalog? Yes if it was.
- `catalog_has_written_description` (yes / no): Does the catalog contain a written description or illustration of the goods or services offered for sale? Yes if it does.
- `catalog_has_seller_address` (yes / no): Does the catalog include the seller's business address? Yes if it does.
- `catalog_has_multiple_pages` (yes / no): Does the catalog include multiple pages of written material or illustrations? Yes if it does.
- `catalog_issued_at_least_yearly` (yes / no): Has the catalog been issued at least once a year? Yes if it has.
- `only_receives_catalog_calls` (yes / no): Does the person making the solicitation never solicit customers by telephone, and only receive calls that customers start in response to the catalog? Yes if so.
- `orders_taken_without_further_solicitation` (yes / no): During those customer-initiated calls, were orders taken without further solicitation? Giving information about, or trying to sell, other items in the same catalog or a substantially similar catalog does not count as further solicitation. Yes if no further solicitation took place.
- `actor_is_seller_or_telemarketer` (yes / no): Is the person whose conduct is under review a seller or a telemarketer? A seller provides, offers to provide, or arranges for others to provide goods or services to the customer for consideration in a telemarketing transaction. A telemarketer starts or receives telephone calls to or from a customer in connection with telemarketing. Yes if the person is either.
- `counterparty_is_customer` (yes / no): Is the person dealt with a customer, meaning a person who is or may be required to pay for the goods or services offered through telemarketing? Yes if so.
- `customer_consented_to_pay` (yes / no): Did the customer consent to pay for the goods or services offered? Yes if they did.
- `total_cost_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the total costs to purchase, receive or use the offered goods or services disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if they were.
- `quantity_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the quantity of the offered goods or services disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if it was.
- `offer_has_material_restrictions` (yes / no): Are there any restrictions, limitations or conditions to purchase, receive or use the offered goods or services that are material, meaning likely to affect a person's choice or conduct? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if there are.
- `material_restrictions_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were all of those material restrictions, limitations and conditions disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if all were.
- `seller_has_no_refund_policy` (yes / no): Does the seller have a policy of not making refunds, cancellations, exchanges or repurchases? Yes if it does.
- `no_refund_policy_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the customer told truthfully, clearly and conspicuously that not making refunds, cancellations, exchanges or repurchases is the seller's policy? Yes if they were.
- `refund_policy_represented` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer make any representation about a refund, cancellation, exchange or repurchase policy? Yes if they did.
- `refund_policy_terms_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were all material terms and conditions of that represented policy disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if they were.
- `offer_includes_prize_promotion` (yes / no): Does the case involve a prize promotion? That means either a sweepstakes or other game of chance, or a representation that a person has won, has been selected to receive, or may be eligible to receive a prize. A prize is anything offered or given by chance, including a guaranteed item that is not identified at the time of the offer. Yes if it does.
- `prize_odds_calculable` (yes / no): Can the odds of being able to receive the prize be calculated in advance? Yes if they can.
- `prize_odds_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the odds of being able to receive the prize disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if they were.
- `prize_odds_factors_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the factors used in calculating the odds disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if they were.
- `no_purchase_required_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was it disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously that no purchase or payment is required to win a prize or to take part in the prize promotion? Yes if it was.
- `purchase_does_not_increase_odds_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was it disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously that any purchase or payment will not increase the person's chances of winning? Yes if it was.
- `no_purchase_method_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the no-purchase/no-payment method of taking part disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? It must come with either instructions on how to take part or an address or local or toll-free telephone number for getting those instructions. Yes if it was.
- `prize_has_material_costs_or_conditions` (yes / no): Are there any material costs or conditions to receive or redeem a prize that is the subject of the prize promotion? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if there are.
- `prize_costs_conditions_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were all those material costs and conditions to receive or redeem the prize disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if all were.
- `goods_represented_to_limit_card_liability` (yes / no): Were the goods or services sold represented to protect, insure or otherwise limit the customer's liability if their credit card is used without authorization? Yes if they were.
- `card_liability_limits_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the limits on a cardholder's liability for unauthorized credit card use under 15 U.S.C. 1643 disclosed truthfully and in a clear and conspicuous manner? Yes if they were.
- `offer_includes_negative_option` (yes / no): Does the offer or agreement include a negative option feature? That is a provision under which the seller treats the customer's silence, or failure to act to reject the goods or services or cancel, as acceptance. Yes if it does.
- `negative_option_charge_fact_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was it disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously that the customer's account will be charged unless they take an affirmative action to avoid the charge? Yes if it was.
- `negative_option_charge_dates_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the dates the charges will be submitted for payment disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Yes if they were.
- `negative_option_avoid_steps_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were the specific steps the customer must take to avoid the charges disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Yes if they were.
- `negative_option_other_terms_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, were all other material terms and conditions of the negative option feature disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Materiality is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if all were.
- `sale_of_debt_relief_service` (yes / no): Is the sale of a debt relief service? That is any program or service represented, directly or by implication, to renegotiate, settle or otherwise alter the terms of debt owed to one or more unsecured creditors or debt collectors, such as reducing the balance, interest or fees. Yes if it is.
- `debt_relief_time_to_results_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the amount of time needed to achieve the represented results disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Yes if it was.
- `debt_relief_may_include_settlement_offer` (yes / no): May the debt relief service include a settlement offer to any of the customer's creditors or debt collectors? Yes if it may.
- `settlement_offer_timing_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the time by which the provider will make a bona fide settlement offer to each creditor or debt collector disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Yes if it was.
- `settlement_accumulation_amount_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the amount of money or percentage of each outstanding debt that the customer must accumulate before the provider makes a bona fide settlement offer disclosed truthfully, clearly and conspicuously? Yes if it was.
- `debt_relief_relies_on_missed_payments` (yes / no): Does any aspect of the debt relief service rely on, or result in, the customer failing to make timely payments to creditors or debt collectors? Yes if it does.
- `missed_payment_consequences_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the customer told truthfully, clearly and conspicuously of all three consequences: the service will likely adversely affect their creditworthiness; it may result in collections or lawsuits by creditors or debt collectors; and it may increase what they owe through fees and interest? Yes only if all three were disclosed.
- `debt_relief_requires_dedicated_account` (yes / no): Does the debt relief service request or require the customer to place funds in an account at an insured financial institution? Yes if it does.
- `dedicated_account_rights_disclosed` (yes / no): Before the customer consented to pay, was the customer told truthfully, clearly and conspicuously of all three rights: they own the funds in the account; they may withdraw from the service at any time without penalty; and on withdrawal they must receive all funds in the account except fees properly earned under 310.4(a)(5)(i)(A)-(C)? Yes only if all three were disclosed.
- `misrepresented_total_cost` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, the total costs to purchase, receive or use the offered goods or services, in a way likely to affect the customer's choice or conduct? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_quantity` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, the quantity of the offered goods or services, in a way likely to affect the customer's choice or conduct? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_material_restriction` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material restriction, limitation or condition to purchase, receive or use the offered goods or services? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_performance_or_nature` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the performance, efficacy, nature or central characteristics of the offered goods or services? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_refund_policy` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the nature or terms of the seller's refund, cancellation, exchange or repurchase policies? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_prize_promotion` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the prize promotion, such as the odds of receiving a prize, the nature or value of a prize, or whether a purchase or payment is required to win or take part? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `offer_is_investment_opportunity` (yes / no): Is what was offered an investment opportunity, meaning anything offered, sold or traded based wholly or partly on express or implied representations about past, present or future income, profit or appreciation? Yes if it is.
- `misrepresented_investment_opportunity` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the investment opportunity, such as risk, liquidity, earnings potential or profitability? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_affiliation_or_endorsement` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, its affiliation with, or endorsement or sponsorship by, any person or government entity? This is a judgement the reviewer applies, including whether the point is material. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_need_for_card_protection` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer represent, directly or by implication, that the customer needs the offered goods or services to get protections they already have under 15 U.S.C. 1643 (limits on cardholder liability for unauthorized credit card use)? Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_negative_option` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the negative option feature? Examples are that the account will be charged unless the customer acts, the charge dates, or the steps needed to avoid the charges. This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so.
- `misrepresented_debt_relief_service` (yes / no): Did the seller or telemarketer misrepresent, directly or by implication, any material aspect of the debt relief service? This is a judgement the reviewer applies. Yes if so. Examples include:
- the amount or percentage the customer may save;
- the time needed to achieve results;
- the amount or percentage that must accumulate before the provider starts negotiating or makes a bona fide offer;
- the effect on creditworthiness or on collection efforts;
- the share or number of customers who attain the represented results;
- whether a non-profit offers or provides the service.
