**Coverage and parties.** An opening line saying the actor "is a telemarketer subject to the Telemarketing Sales Rule" settles several facts:
- induces_purchase_by_telephone, more_than_one_interstate_call, actor_is_seller_or_telemarketer and counterparty_is_customer are yes.
- solicited_through_mailed_catalog is no, unless a catalog is described.

**Consent.** customer_consented_to_pay is yes when the record says the customer bought, agreed, joined, was "sold" or "convinced," or is on the seller's plan. It is no only if the record says the customer declined or paid nothing, as in a free trial that ends unless the customer later opts in.

**Silence.** These records are short and state the defect they raise. Read silence as follows:
- A topic never mentioned (prize, negative option, debt relief, investment, card protection, refund policy) is absent. Its trigger fact is no.
- A misrepresentation fact is no unless the record recounts a statement or description at odds with the truth.
- A required disclosure the record does not call missing, late or vague is yes.
- Fill every fact. Do not leave triggers unknown.

**Disclosure quality.**
- A disclosure is no if the record says it was omitted, given only after the sale, or given in vague terms ("a handful," "a very reasonable price," "a few months or so").
- Concrete terms ("4 brooms for $10," "within 90 days," "exchanges but not refunds") are yes.

**Price.**
- A stated price with an undisclosed extra charge needed to buy, receive or use the goods means total_cost_disclosed is no. Examples are shipping, fees and required parts.
- Quoting a price as if it were complete also misrepresents total cost by implication.
- Stating before consent that an item or fee is excluded from the price discloses the cost truthfully.

**Restrictions and nature.**
- A condition limiting when or how the goods can be used is a material restriction.
- Omitted trivia that does not limit use or change what is sold is neither a material restriction nor a misrepresentation. Examples are origin and the domestic share of goods.
- A description the customer would naturally read as something other than what is delivered misrepresents nature by implication.

**Negative option.**
- Automatic renewal or charges "until cancelled" is a negative option. "Automatically renews monthly after the trial" discloses the charge dates.
- An undisclosed cost of cancelling means negative_option_other_terms_disclosed is no.
- A cancellation method the record states, however burdensome, means negative_option_avoid_steps_disclosed is yes.
