Try Resolve

Everything here runs in your browser. A Dafny-proved evaluator decides each case, and Z3 checks the rules. The SOP is a Know Your Business (KYB) procedure: the checks a bank runs to confirm who owns and controls a company before opening its account. The six cases are made up. The only recorded part is the wording of the SOP owner's questions, which Claude wrote on 29 Oct 2026.

1 · The rules

Check, answer, write the next version

Z3 looks at every possible case for rules that can never apply, rules that treat an unknown differently from SOP 2.2 (the unknowns rule), and facts that never change an outcome.

Six cases under the current version
