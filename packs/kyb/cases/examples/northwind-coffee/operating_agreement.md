# Operating Agreement of Northwind Coffee Co. LLC (excerpt)

Amended and restated 2024-01-08.

**Page 1 of 4**

1.1 The name of the Company is Northwind Coffee Co. LLC, an Oregon limited liability company.
1.2 The Company's purpose is the roasting, packaging and wholesale distribution of coffee.

**Page 2 of 4**

4.1 Units. The Company has issued 1,000 common units. The Members and their units are listed in Schedule A.
4.2 No Member may transfer units without the written consent of the Managing Member.

**Page 3 of 4**

6.1 Management. The Company is manager-managed. Dana Ruiz, Managing Member, has full authority to manage and direct the business and affairs of the Company, including opening bank accounts and signing contracts.
6.2 Halcyon Holdings LLC and Wei Lee are passive members and take no part in day-to-day management.

**Page 4 of 4**

Schedule A. Dana Ruiz, 350 units. Halcyon Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 400 units. Wei Lee, 250 units.
